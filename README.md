## My Favorite Series: *Attack on Titan*

> “I Want To Be Her One and Only For the Rest of My Life!” – Eren Yeager

### Why I love it
*Attack on Titan* is an intense, dark, and beautifully animated anime that keeps you on the edge of your seat. The story explores freedom, war, and the cycle of hatred in a way that few series dare to.
